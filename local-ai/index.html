THE WORKBENCH

Local AI

An Android app for running AI models fully on your phone. No cloud, no account, nothing leaves the device.

IN PROGRESS ANDROID OFFLINE
THE IDEA

Most AI apps are a thin window onto someone else's server. Every message you type goes off to a data centre, gets logged somewhere, and stops working the moment you lose signal.

Local AI flips that around. The model lives on your phone and runs on your phone. You can ask it things in airplane mode, in a dead zone or at a table in the back of a tavern, and the only one reading your chats is you.

WHAT IT'S FOR
📵

Works offline

Once a model is on the device, no internet is needed to chat with it.

🔒

Private by default

Prompts and replies stay on the phone. No servers in the middle, no telemetry on your conversations.

🚪

No account

Install it and start. No sign-up, no email, no subscription.

⚗️

Your choice of model

Pick a model that fits your phone, from small and quick to bigger and smarter.

QUESTIONS AT THE BAR
When can I get it?

It isn't released yet. Updates will land here and on stream first.

Does it really never go online?

Chatting happens entirely on the device. The app has to get a model onto the phone somehow, so that step needs a connection, but your conversations themselves never leave the phone.

Will it run on my phone?

AI models are hungry for memory, so newer phones with more RAM will run larger models. Smaller models are there for older devices.

iPhone?

Android first. No iOS plans right now.
