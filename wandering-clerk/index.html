THE IDEA

Most chat games are a single command and a random number. Hunt & Hoard is a whole loop. Every viewer gets a character in the Clerk's ledger, with a race, a class, HP, XP, gold and a pack full of gear that sticks around between streams.

From there it's the classic adventurer's day: take a bounty off the board, go hunting, come back battered, rest up, spend the loot at the merchant's stall and do it all again. The Clerk keeps the books and narrates every step in chat, and every reply ends with a hint at what to do next.

A DAY AT THE GUILD
viewer: !enlist random
WanderingClerk: @viewer the quill scratches across the ledger — Kestrel the Wayward is sworn in as a Level 1 Halfling Rogue! …
viewer: !quests
WanderingClerk: 📜 The bounty board reads: #1 Hunt 6 Giant Rats (0/6) → 17 gold | #2 … Say !hunt <monster name> to take up a bounty.
viewer: !hunt giant rat
WanderingClerk: Kestrel the Wayward crossed blades with a Giant Rat and prevailed! +10 XP, +2 gold. HP 7/8. 📜 Bounty progress: Giant Rat 1/6.
WHAT'S IN THE LEDGER
🪪

Characters

Pick a name or let the Clerk roll one. You're sworn in as one of 8 races and 8 classes, from Halfling Rogue to Dragonborn Paladin, and level up as the XP rolls in.

⚔️

Hunting

Over 40 monsters, from Giant Rats to an Ancient Red Dragon. Fights are rolled with real dice, attack bonuses and armor class, and foes are matched to your level unless you name your own.

⏳

Autohunt

Send your character out for anywhere from 5 minutes to 2 hours. They fight a bout every few minutes, rest when HP runs low and report back with a tally.

📜

Bounty board

Three shared bounties at a time. The first hunter to hit the kill count claims the gold (and sometimes an item), and a fresh posting goes up. The whole board turns over every 20 to 30 minutes.

🛒

The merchant's stall

Nearly 40 potions, weapons, armor and trinkets. Buy, equip, drink or sell back at half price. Each slot restocks the moment it's bought.

🔥

Healing by the fire

!rest patches you up to 80%, and characters slowly heal on their own between fights, even between streams.

THE CHARTER OF COMMANDS

🪪 Getting started
  • !start where you stand and what to do next
  • !enlist <name> or !enlist random
  • !ledger your sheet, or !ledger @user (also !chars)
  • !discharge confirm retire your character
  • !help the full guide

⚔️ Out in the field
  • !hunt or !hunt <monster>
  • !autohunt 20m (also !auto)
  • !autostatus and !autostop
  • !rest recover between bouts
  • !quests the bounty board (also !board)

🛒 Gold & gear
  • !merchant today's wares (also !shop)
  • !buy 1 or !buy <item>
  • !inventory (also !inv) and !purse
  • !use, !sell and !drop <item>
  • !item <name> look up any ware

🎲 Just for fun
  • !d20 or !d20 @user (also !roll)
  • !lurk slip into the shadows, still marked present
QUESTIONS AT THE BAR
Does it spam chat?

No. Besides answering commands, the Clerk only posts the odd announcement (new stall wares, fresh bounties, a bit of item lore, a nudge to !start), only while the channel is live, and never more than once per 30 minutes or 5 chat messages.

Can streamers turn parts of it off?

Yes. The broadcaster and mods get a back-room panel with toggles for characters, combat, the shop, quests, dice and each kind of announcement.

Is my character the same in every channel?

Yes. Your character is tied to your Twitch account, so the same adventurer follows you to any channel the Clerk works in.

How is it built?

A Deno bot connects to Twitch's EventSub chat feed and answers commands, while a Val Town backend stores the ledger, the board and the stall, and hosts the command guide and admin panel. It was built and tested with TwitchBotSandbox.

Can I add it to my channel?

Not yet. It's still being finished on stream. Keep an eye on this page.
