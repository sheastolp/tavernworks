Wake in Verrowen

You wake up in a magic land called Verrowen, and you can't remember anything. Not even your name! A storyteller helps you explore. Look for clues, and find out who you really are.

THE STORY

How it works

  1. Press Begin. The storyteller tells you where you are.
  2. Tap 🔊 to hear the story read out loud. Tap 🎤 to say your answer instead of typing it.
  3. Tell the storyteller what you do or say. You can pick one of the ideas, or do anything you like!
  4. Look for clues about who you are. When you're done, press Finish.
✨ A new character for the Grand Orrery. You don't know anything about them yet, not even their name. You find it all out by playing! When you finish, Sheamus gets to see them, and they show up on the Orrery's list while he decides. Just play, without sending them

About the world. Verrowen is a land of castles, small everyday magic and monsters, some grumpy and some friendly. Stories can start anywhere in it: the Kingdom's busy capital, the long roads, the river and the lake, the mountains, or Stoutwell, a walled village of humans and halflings next to the Monster Wood. Grown-ups can read all about the world on GitHub.

    
Who is the storyteller? The storyteller is a computer program (an AI) that lives on Sheamus's laptop, the same one as Local AI. If the laptop is asleep, the storyteller can't answer. Each answer takes a few seconds.
