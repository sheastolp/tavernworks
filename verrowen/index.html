Wake in Verrowen

Make a character by playing them. You wake with no memory, not even your name, somewhere in the world of Verrowen. A narrator guides you, and your choices decide who you are: your race, your past, your magic. Your name turns up along the way.

THE STORY

How it works

  1. Press Begin. The narrator sets the first scene.
  2. Tap 🔊 to hear the story read aloud, and 🎤 to say your answer instead of typing it.
  3. Reply however you like: what you do, say, notice or think.
  4. When your character feels complete, press Wrap it up and choose a story or a D&D character sheet.

Just a first name or a nickname is plenty.

✨ A new character for the Grand Orrery. Nothing is picked in advance: no name, no race, no backstory. Everything about them, from who they are to their past and their magic, comes out of how you play. When you wrap it up, they're sent for review and show up on the Orrery's roster as pending until the owner decides. Play without sending them in

About the world. Verrowen is part of the Grand Orrery, a medieval fantasy universe of weak, everyday magic, D&D-style monsters and a far-off, corrupt Kingdom. Most stories brush against Stoutwell, a walled village of humans and halflings on the edge of the Monster Wood. Read the whole universe on GitHub.

    
How it runs. The narrator is a small AI model on the same laptop as Local AI. Your browser talks to it directly, so nothing goes through a cloud AI service. If the laptop is asleep, the narrator can't answer. Replies take a few seconds.
