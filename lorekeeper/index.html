THE WORKBENCH

Lorekeeper

A Windows app that turns your VODs, recordings, screenshots and docs into a library you can ask questions about, with a built-in video editor and a clip finder that knows people by voice.

BETA WINDOWS RUNS OFFLINE NO ACCOUNTS
📦 How to install Source on GitHub
THE IDEA

Hours of VODs, a folder of screenshots and a pile of PDFs are hard to search. Lorekeeper reads, watches and listens to all of it, then answers questions with clickable sources that jump to the exact page, passage or video timestamp.

The AI ships inside the app and runs on your PC: Gemma 4 for answers and vision, EmbeddingGemma for search and Whisper for transcripts. No Ollama, no servers, no accounts, and nothing leaves your machine.

WHAT'S IN THE BOX
🗂️

Library

Drag in video, audio, images, PDFs (scanned ones too), Word, PowerPoint, text and web links. Files are indexed where they are and never changed.

❓

Ask

Every answer comes in four parts, The Answer, The How, The Why and a Deep Cut, with gold citation numbers you can click.

🎬

Clips by voice

Teach it a person once and it finds them in your VODs by voice, scores their best moments and cuts them into 16:9 or vertical clips with word-by-word captions. Pulls VODs straight from Twitch.

✂️

Edit by transcript

Strike words to cut them from the video, auto-strike filler words, cut dead air and export SRT or VTT captions that match your cuts.

🎛️

Studio & timeline

Trim, split, merge, speed, denoise, loudness fixes and conversions, plus a timeline with a facecam overlay track, two audio tracks, fades and undo.

🎮

Plays nice with games

Uses NVIDIA, AMD or Intel GPUs, or just the CPU. The model unloads when idle so your games get the VRAM back.

DOWNLOAD & INSTALL

GitHub builds a fresh Lorekeeper installer every time the app is updated. You download the newest one and run it. All you need is a free GitHub account, because GitHub only lets signed-in people download builds. Plan on a few minutes for the installer and a one-time AI model download of 3 to 5 GB.

  1. Sign in to GitHub

    Sign in at github.com, or make a free account first.

  2. Open the latest build

    Open Lorekeeper's finished builds. That's the Actions tab, filtered to Build Windows installer runs that worked. Click the run at the top of the list; it's the newest.

  3. Download the installer

    Scroll to Artifacts at the bottom of the run and click Lorekeeper-Setup-x.y.z. A zip file downloads.

    Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. Inside is Lorekeeper-Setup-x.y.z.exe.

  4. Run the installer

    Double-click Lorekeeper-Setup-x.y.z.exe. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway. That shows up because the app isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate, not because anything's wrong.

    It installs just for you, so there's no admin prompt. Its files go in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Lorekeeper.

  5. Pick an answer model

    The installer asks which AI model to use for answers. Leave it on Pick the best fit for this PC automatically unless you know you want one of these:

    • Gemma 4 E4B: best answers. 4.8 GB download, wants 12 GB+ of RAM or a GPU with 6 GB+.
    • Gemma 4 E2B: lighter and faster, same vision skills. 3.2 GB download, runs on 8 GB of RAM or a 4 GB GPU.

    Tick Create a desktop shortcut if you want one, then finish the installer.

  6. First launch

    Open Lorekeeper from the Start menu. The first time, it downloads the model you picked, with a progress screen. If your connection drops, it picks up where it left off. Search, transcripts and editing work straight away; questions work as soon as the model lands.

    After that, everything runs offline.

Updating: download the newest build the same way (steps 2 to 4) and run the installer over the old one. Your library, models and settings stay.
Needs: Windows 10 or 11, 8 GB of RAM at the least (12 GB+ for the bigger model), and free disk space for the app plus a 3 to 5 GB model. A GPU helps but isn't required.
No Artifacts on a run? GitHub deletes old builds after a while. Pick a newer run, or ask in the Discord for a fresh build.
Where your stuff lives: your library, downloaded models and settings are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lorekeeper, and every render goes to Videos\Lorekeeper Exports.
The README covers clips, the watermark and running from source too.
QUESTIONS AT THE BAR
Why isn't the AI model inside the installer?

GitHub caps downloads at 2 GB per file and the model alone is bigger than that. Everything else, the AI engine, the search model, Whisper and FFmpeg, is inside. The model downloads once on first launch and is checked so a broken download can't sneak through.

I already have GGUF models. Can I use those?

Yes. Settings → AI → Add a GGUF file… uses them where they are, with nothing copied. Pick the model and its mmproj-*.gguf together to give it vision.

Can it use my Ollama server instead?

Yes. Settings → AI → Ollama server sends every AI call to Ollama, on this PC or another one on your network.

Will it slow down my games?

Only while it's thinking. Free memory when idle (10 minutes by default) unloads the model so your games get the VRAM back, and it reloads on the next question.

Why is there a tavernworks.dev tag on my exports?

Lorekeeper puts a small watermark in one corner of finished videos. Change the text, corner and size, or switch it off, in Settings → Watermark.

Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now. Say so in the Discord if you want it elsewhere.
