SET IT UP

1Make sure GuildScribe is in your channel

Sound Bytes rides on GuildScribe's custom commands. If GuildScribe isn't in your chat yet, raise the banner here.

2Get your overlay link

Type your Twitch channel name. The link updates as you change the settings.

Type your channel name above
▶ Open a test (plays the Nat 20 fanfare)

3Add it to OBS

  1. In your scene, add a Browser source and paste the link as the URL.
  2. Set the size to 1920 × 1080 (or your canvas size). The page is see-through until a sound plays.
  3. Tick Control audio via OBS so the sounds show up in your mixer.

4Teach GuildScribe the commands

Paste these into your Twitch chat as the broadcaster or a mod, one at a time. Each one makes a !command that anyone in chat can use. Edit the wording after the sound name however you like; just keep 🔊 name at the very start.

Fireball is a trigger rather than a command: anyone saying “fireball” anywhere in chat sets it off (it also casts the spell in GuildScribe's Endless Delve). Then add cooldowns so chat can't spam them, for example !dndbot cooldown nat20 30 for 30 seconds, or !trigger cooldown fireball 30 for the fireball.

QUESTIONS AT THE BAR
How does it work?

The overlay reads your chat the same way a viewer does, without logging in. When GuildScribe posts a message that starts with 🔊 and a sound name, the overlay plays that sound. Since only GuildScribe's messages count, GuildScribe's cooldowns decide how often a sound can go off.

Can I name the commands something else?

Yes. The command name is yours to pick; only the word after 🔊 chooses the sound. !dndbot add crit 🔊 nat20 CRITICAL HIT from {user}! plays the Nat 20 fanfare on !crit.

Can a sound play without a command?

Yes, with a GuildScribe trigger, which fires on a word anywhere in chat: !trigger add gg 🔊 coins {user} tips the bard! Give it a cooldown with !trigger cooldown gg 60.

What if a lot of people fire sounds at once?

Sounds wait their turn and play one after another. Up to five can wait in line; anything past that is skipped so the stream doesn't turn into a cacophony.

Can I hide the on-screen card?

Untick Show a card on screen above before copying the link, and the overlay plays sound only.

Nothing plays in OBS. What do I check?

Open the test link above in OBS's browser source first: if the fanfare plays, the audio side works. Then make sure your GuildScribe command's response starts with 🔊, and that the sound name is one from the soundboard. Add &debug=1 to the link to keep the connection status on screen. If you use your own bot account for GuildScribe, add &bot=yourbotname.
