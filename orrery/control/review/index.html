Review Clone

Every change to the Grand Orrery waits here first, on grand-orrery's review branch. Nothing reaches the live Orrery, the lore-keeper or the Wake in Verrowen narrator until you publish it.

What is the review?

The review is everything waiting to go live, taken together: your edits, lore-keeper write-ups, Codex notes and Wake in Verrowen characters, each one filed on the review branch as you send or accept it. The Review Clone is the whole Orrery Explorer read from that branch, so you can browse it exactly as it will look.

✔ Publish puts all of it live at once: the Explorer, the lore-keeper and the narrator read it from then on. Throw it all away drops all of it and lines the clone back up with what's live. To keep some and drop the rest, edit the files on the review branch first. A (Pitch) stays an idea even once published; only (Your idea) is canon.

😴 Rest mode · 🤖 Auto mode

While you rest, the laptop's AI runs the Orrery on its own. Every round it judges everything sent in for review and every pitch on the review branch against the lore (approved ones become Your idea, rejected ones are turned down or taken out), comes up with new ideas of its own a few times a day and judges each one before filing it (only the ones it accepts are kept, as Your idea; the rest are only listed below), catches lore.md up for the narrator, and publishes it all to the live Orrery and GuildScribe. Anything the AI can't give a clear answer on waits here for you. Every decision and its reason is listed below and in the change's message on GitHub.

🤖 Auto mode does all the same work but never publishes: nothing gets decided for you. Every round writes a new idea (no daily limit) and files it as a pitch with its view, and what it approves from the 📨 queue is filed as a pitch too. Pitches stay pitches: they all wait in 💡 Pitches with the AI's view (would accept or would reject, and why) for you to decide. What it would turn down from the 📨 queue stays there with its reason. You check it and publish (or throw it away) as usual.

What rest mode has done

    The owner's token (under Waiting for you) is needed. Something it published by mistake can be put back by hand on GitHub or with a pitch.

    💡 Pitches

    Every (Pitch) in working-notes.md, resources.md and bestiary.md on the review branch. ✔ Accept as is keeps its words exactly and only marks it (Your idea). ✕ Reject takes it out of the file (a heading pitch with its whole section). Either one is a single change on the review branch: it joins "Waiting for you" below and goes live when you publish, and Throw it all away undoes it. To change the words first, use Edit.

    🤖 Added by the AI

    Everything rest mode or auto mode put in working-notes.md on the review branch: its own ideas, and what it filed from the 📨 queue, whether still a Pitch or accepted as Your idea. If one turns out to be wrong, 🗑 Delete takes its whole section out as one change on the review branch. Publish to take it off the live Orrery too.

    ✨ Add to the Orrery

    Say what you'd like to add, in your own words, and the laptop's local AI writes it into every file it belongs in: a line for the narrator in lore.md, a row in the roster, a section of the universe draft, an entry in resources.md or a creature in the bestiary. Check and edit every part, add or drop parts, then add it as Your idea or file it as a pitch. It goes on the review branch like everything else. You can also skip the AI and write the parts yourself.

    📜 Lore catch-up

    Keeps lore.md, what the Wake in Verrowen narrator reads, in step with what you've decided. It finds every (Your idea) section in the notes, resources.md and bestiary.md that's new or changed since the last catch-up, and the laptop's AI drafts the lines the lore is missing. Pitches never go in. Check and edit the draft, then add it: it goes on the review branch like everything else.

      If a line corrects something the lore already says, also fix the old line with Edit lore.md.

      📚 Documents catch-up

      The other way round from the lore catch-up: lore.md is the decided canon, and this brings the documents in line with it. The laptop's AI reads universe.md, roster.md, resources.md, bestiary.md and the decided parts of the working notes, and drafts fixes where they contradict the lore, facts the universe draft is missing, new item and resource entries for things the lore names that have none, and a list of places no map shows yet. It runs on the laptop in the background, so you can leave the page; players always go first, so it can take a while. Nothing changes until you tick what to keep and apply it: one change per file on the review branch, which you publish as usual.

      🗺 Maps

      Open a map, click where something goes, name it and pick a symbol, and add it. Marks you added can be taken off again. Save the map on review keeps the map's editable SVG and redraws its picture from it, in your browser. You can also start a new map, which the Explorer lists on its own.
