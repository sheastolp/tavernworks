Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

The short version

Tavernworks is a small hobby site run by Stoned Sheamus. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only personal information it keeps is what you choose to give it, and you can delete it.

Signing in with Google (TwitchBotSandbox)

The live TwitchBotSandbox lets you sign in with Google to save your work. When you do, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us, along with an account ID. We don't ask for, and can't see, anything else in your Google account: no Gmail, Drive, contacts or password.

We use that information only to keep your projects in your own account and to show you who you're signed in as.

What we store

  • Your sandbox projects: the files, chat history and layout you create in the sandbox, plus automatic backups (up to 30).
  • GitHub or Val Town tokens, if you save one to sync your projects. These are stored encrypted and are only used when you click sync.
  • Your name, email and picture from Google, and when you last signed in.
  • Hushwave suggestions: the name (optional) and message you send in the suggestion box.

If you use the sandbox without signing in, your projects stay in your own browser and are never sent to us.

Where it's kept

Everything is stored on a single computer run by Tavernworks, not a third-party cloud. It isn't sold, shared or used for advertising. Our server also sees your IP address on each request, which it uses only in memory to stop spam and repeated sign-in attempts; it isn't saved.

When you sync with GitHub or Val Town, your browser talks to those services directly, under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

In the sandbox, open Backups… → Delete my data. That removes your projects, tokens, backups and Google profile details from our server immediately. You can also remove Tavernworks' access in your Google account settings.

Cookies and storage

We don't use cookies. The sandbox uses your browser's local storage to remember your sign-in, or your projects if you aren't signed in. Google's sign-in button may set its own cookies when you use it.

Children

Tavernworks isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Questions or deletion requests: ask in the Tavernworks Discord.
