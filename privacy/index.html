Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

Tavernworks (tavernworks.dev) is a small set of tools for streamers, run by one person. This page explains what the site keeps about you and what it doesn't. In short: only what's needed to save your work, never sold, never used for ads, and you can delete it yourself.

Signing in with Google

The live TwitchBotSandbox lets you sign in with Google so your projects follow you between browsers. When you do, Google shares with us:

  • your Google account ID (used to find your saved projects),
  • your name, email address and profile picture (shown in the app so you know which account you're signed in with).

We ask Google for nothing else: no access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data. Signing in is optional; without it, projects stay in your browser only.

What we store

  • Your sandbox projects, layout preferences and automatic backups (the newest 30 are kept).
  • Tokens you choose to save, such as GitHub or Val Town tokens used to publish your bots. These are encrypted at rest and only sent back to you when you're signed in.
  • Suggestions you send through a suggestion box, exactly as you write them.

The server also sees your IP address on each request. It's used in memory to rate-limit sign-ins and spam and isn't saved with your account.

How it's used and shared

Your data is used only to run the features you use. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it to track you across other sites. The site has no ads and no analytics. Data is sent to a third party only when you ask for it, for example publishing a bot to GitHub or Val Town with your own token.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it's kept

Saved data lives on a server operated by Tavernworks. Pages are delivered through a content delivery network, which may keep standard request logs.

Deleting your data

In TwitchBotSandbox, open Backups… and choose Delete my data. That permanently removes your projects, tokens, backups and profile from the server right away. You can also remove Tavernworks' access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Children

Tavernworks isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions or deletion requests can go through the suggestion box.
