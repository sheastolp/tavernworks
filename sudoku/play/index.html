How to play

Type a cell then a number in chat:

B7 4

No space needed: B74 works too, and so does b7=4. Right answer +10, finish a row / column / box +5 each, wrong −2 and a short timeout.

✏️ Notes: put ? after the cell, then the numbers. No points. More than one cell at once: A1A3?67

, like the dashed cell , which always shows what can still go there.

B7?-3 rubs out 3 · B7?x clears.
