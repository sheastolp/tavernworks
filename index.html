    The Wandering Clerk

    Guild receptionist for GuildBreak: Hunt & Hoard, a hunt-and-loot RPG that runs right in Twitch chat. Enlist a character, hunt monsters, claim bounties and spend your gold at the merchant's stall.

    tavernworks.dev/wandering-clerkLIVE
    🥸

    UndercoverBurn

    Slips into your Twitch chat undercover, then blows its cover with a playful roast, a quote from your StreamElements !quote list, or a one-liner riffing on what chat is saying. Also calls out ad breaks and welcomes chat back. Only talks while you're live.

    THE JOURNAL

    📖 Adventurer's Journal BETA

    A campaign notebook that lives in your browser. Keep a project for every campaign, one-shot or character, and fill it with numbered session notes.

    • Projects with numbered entries, add or remove as you go
    • Line-numbered editor with upload and download (.md, .txt, .html)
    • Attach maps, handouts and portraits with inline image previews
    • Image Forge: scene art from your own ComfyUI, saved to the entry
