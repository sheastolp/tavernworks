Link Your Launchers

Link Steam, the Epic Games Launcher, GOG Galaxy, the Xbox app and Microsoft Store, or everything installed on Windows, and we'll find the games (and apps) you have installed, then build a companion app for each one: launch button, goals, notes, a play-session log and the best guides and wikis. Games with a hand-built assistant open that instead.

Your browser reads the launchers' install records on your own PC. The list stays in this browser and is never uploaded to us. Turn on Sync with Google at the bottom to keep it, and your companions' notes, the same in your other browsers.

What the script does

      
It only reads: Steam's appmanifest_*.acf files in every library, Epic's .item manifests in ProgramData, GOG Galaxy's game list in the registry, your Microsoft Store and Xbox app packages (Get-AppxPackage), and the programs listed in Add or remove programs (the registry's Uninstall keys). Runtimes, drivers and updates are left out. It changes nothing and sends nothing anywhere; the result goes to your clipboard.
